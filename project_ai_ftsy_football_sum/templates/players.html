The player reference

Every player on an NFL depth chart, as summaries are written against them. Depth rank is where a player sits on their team’s depth chart — who is on the field. ECR tier is where their expert consensus ranking falls — who is worth starting. A starting kicker is depth rank 1 and a bottom ECR tier; the two do not imply one another.
